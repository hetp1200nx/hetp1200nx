Where vacuum does the work.
And what keeps it working.

How I look at an application

Horsepower and price come last. The process comes first.

Walk the process

What's in the vessel, what leaves it as vapour, how hot, how long the cycle runs.

Measure the duty

Working vacuum, pumping speed, gas and condensable load, corrosiveness, running hours.

Match the pump

Oil-sealed, dry, booster-assisted: and the condenser, trap or filter that protects it.

Keep it running

Right spares in stock, planned overhauls, and an AMC if the pump is critical.

03

Support across the pump's life

Through Mechvac and VacSolutë, for any brand already installed in your plant.

Spares & kits

Vanes, bearings, seals, filters, rotors and overhaul kits for rotary vane, dry screw and claw pumps, to the OEM part number.

Repair & overhaul

Workshop or on-site. Tested before and after, with a failure report so the same fault doesn't return.

AMC / CMC

Scheduled visits, a status report each time, and priority breakdown support for critical pumps.

Inspection & audit

A one-time check of installed pumps: vacuum, current, temperature, noise, with clear recommendations.

Rotor coatings

PEEK, PTFE, PFA, electroless nickel and Xylan for dry screw pumps in corrosive service.

Replacement pumps

When repair no longer pays: a direct Mechvac equivalent for the installed pump, sized to the real duty.
